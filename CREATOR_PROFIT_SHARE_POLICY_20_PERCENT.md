# BharatVarshOfficial Creator Profit Share Policy — 20%

Updated: 2026-09-03

## Core rule

A monetized creator receives **20% of verified net platform profit that BharatVarshOfficial attributes to that creator's eligible content/activity**.

Formula:

`Creator earning = Verified creator-attributed platform profit × 20%`

Examples:
- ₹100 verified attributed profit → creator earns ₹20.
- ₹1,000 verified attributed profit → creator earns ₹200.
- ₹10,000 verified attributed profit → creator earns ₹2,000.

## What can contribute to attributed profit

Eligible creator-driven activity can include monetized views, wallpaper/image downloads, video views, ad impressions, sponsorship income, premium/platform income and other legitimate revenue connected to that creator's public content.

Only verified net profit is settled. Invalid/bot/fraudulent traffic and unverified revenue must not be counted.

## Security

Creators cannot directly edit earnings, profit attribution, the 20% share rate, or payout balances. Financial settlement remains admin/trusted-backend controlled in Firestore.

The current admin dashboard accepts **verified creator-attributed platform profit**, automatically calculates exactly **20%**, credits only that creator share, and records a profit-share transaction. A future ads/payment analytics backend can feed the same verified profit settlement flow automatically without changing the 20% rule.

## Payout

Current minimum payout remains ₹1,000 unless the platform policy is changed later.
