About, Privacy & Terms

Last updated: 24 August 2026

About Us

BharatVarshOfficial is a digital wallpaper platform celebrating India's history, heroes, defence forces, temples, festivals, culture and natural beauty. Our goal is to make respectful, high-quality wallpapers easy to discover and download.

Approved creators may submit original media for review. Submitted media remains private until an administrator approves and publishes it. Monetisation is not active.

Privacy Policy

Information we process

If you use Google sign-in, the site may store your Firebase user identifier, display name, email address, profile photo URL, favourites and download count. If you subscribe to the newsletter, the email address you submit is stored for that purpose.

How information is used

  • To sign you in and display your account.
  • To save favourites and basic download history totals.
  • To operate and protect the website.
  • To send wallpaper updates when newsletter delivery is enabled.

Storage and third parties

Authentication and database records are handled through Firebase. The website is delivered through GitHub Pages. Media hosted by an external provider may be requested from that provider when it is displayed or downloaded. Theme preference is stored locally in your browser.

Your choices

You may use public browsing and downloads without signing in. Favourites require sign-in. A verified privacy contact and account deletion workflow will be published before public creator submissions are enabled. Do not send sensitive personal information through wallpaper titles, descriptions or public links.

Terms of Use

Use the website lawfully and respectfully. Do not attempt to bypass security controls, automate abusive traffic, manipulate counters, upload malware, impersonate another person or submit content that violates privacy, intellectual-property or other legal rights.

Wallpapers are provided for the use stated by their owner or uploader. Availability, metadata and external media links may change. The service may remove content, restrict access or update these terms to protect users and the platform.

Creator earnings, targets or monetisation are not active unless the website explicitly displays an official, verified programme with separate eligibility and payment terms.

Copyright and Content Rights

Upload or submit only content you created, licensed or have permission to share. Giving credit does not replace permission. Content may be removed when ownership or permission cannot be verified.

Copyright concerns should be raised through the platform's designated support channel with the media URL, the reason for the report and evidence of ownership or permission. Reported content may be hidden while it is reviewed.
